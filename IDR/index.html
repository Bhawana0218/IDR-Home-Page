About IDR

The Institute of Digital Risk (IDR) is an industry-led training and deployment institute focused on digital, cyber, and technology risk roles in regulated, high-consequence environments.

Through our strategic partnership with leading UK universities, we bridge the gap between theoretical academic insight and practical real-world application. We prepare the next generation of professionals to navigate the complex landscape of AI governance and digital resilience.

Our Model

Academy

Intensive bootcamps and training programs designed for students and professionals to upskill in digital and cyber risk management.

Innovation & Incubation

Developing proprietary IP, future risk models, and AI governance frameworks to stay ahead of emerging technological threats.

Advisory Services

Expert consultation helping organizations implement robust frameworks like NIST, ISO 27001, and NIS2 for resilient operations.
